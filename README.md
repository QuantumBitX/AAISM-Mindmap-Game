# AAISM Map Drill

A drag-and-drop game for memorising the 17 AAISM mind maps by rebuilding each one from memory.

**[Play it here](https://quantumbitx.github.io/AAISM-Mindmap-Game/)**

## How to play

Drag each term from the tray onto its branch, or tap a term and then tap the spot where it belongs. A wrong drop counts as a miss. Miss the same term twice and the game shows you where it goes.

| Level | What's blank |
|---|---|
| Easy | About half the outer terms |
| Medium | Every outer term, with branch headings left in place |
| Hard | The whole map apart from its title |

- **Study** shows the full map so you can read it through first.
- **Hint** highlights the right spot for a term. It counts against your score.
- **Swap terms** deals a different set of terms from the deck.

Each map earns up to three ticks: three for a clean run with no misses or hints, two when at least 85% of terms land first time, and one for finishing. Your best ticks and times are saved in your browser.

## Maps covered

| Domain | Maps |
|---|---|
| 1. AI Governance and Program Management | Structure, Oversight and Stakeholders · AI Regulation, Standards, and Frameworks · AI Strategy Components and Implications · AI Limitations and Ethical Use · Data Lifecycle and Quality Management · AI Security · AI Incident Response |
| 2. AI Risk Management | AI Risk Management · AI Threats and Vulnerabilities · AI Supply Chain |
| 3. AI Technologies and Controls | AI Architecture & Foundations · AI Algorithm Classes & Security Architecture · AI Life Cycle · Data Management Controls · Privacy and Safety Controls · Security Controls · Other Controls and Metrics |

Map 2.3, AI Supply Chain, swaps the source slide's Deployers and Providers headings to match the EU AI Act. The Study view explains the change.

## Running it

The game is a single `index.html` file with no build step. Open it in a browser, or host it on any static site host such as GitHub Pages.
